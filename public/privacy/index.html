This policy explains what information TagPulse AI handles, why, and which service providers are involved. It describes how the service works today; if the service changes, we will update this page.

1. Who we are

TagPulse AI (“TagPulse AI”, “we”, “us”) is an AI-assisted tool that helps online sellers create listing titles, tags and descriptions. The service is operated by Maryam, the legal account holder for TagPulse AI. TagPulse AI is the product name of the service.

Website: https://tag-pulse-ai.pages.dev/

2. Information we collect

Account information

To use the generator you sign in with a Google account or a secure email link. We receive your email address and an account identifier from our authentication provider (Supabase). We do not ask you to create a password.

Product and keyword inputs

The keyword, category, audience, SEO goal and any optional product details or existing listing text you type into the tool are used to build the request that generates your results.

Generated content

When a generation completes, our server records the generated result against a request identifier and your account identifier. This is used so that a repeated or interrupted request can return the same result without being charged twice. We do not currently apply an automatic deletion schedule to these records (see Retention).

Credit, usage and purchase records

Our database stores, linked to your account identifier: your remaining free or paid credit balance, a record that a generation took place (account identifier and timestamp, not the text), and, for paid plans, license and purchase details such as a hashed license key (we do not store the license key itself in readable form), its status, the credits granted and used, and product or order identifiers supplied by the payment provider.

Feedback you send

If you use the feedback form, your message, the feedback type and the optional email address you enter are sent to us by email through an email delivery provider. We do not store the message in our database. To limit abuse we store a salted, hashed form of your network address with a timestamp, not the address itself.

Technical information

Our hosting platform and server functions generate technical logs, for example request identifiers, timings, error codes and the length (not the text) of a request. We do not intentionally write the text of your inputs to our logs.

3. How your information flows

  1. You sign in. Your browser talks to Supabase to authenticate you and keeps a session.
  2. You submit a generation. Your browser sends the request, with your session token, to our server functions on Cloudflare.
  3. Our server verifies your token with Supabase, checks and reserves a credit in our Cloudflare D1 database, and sends the prompt containing your inputs to our AI provider, Groq.
  4. The AI result returns to our server, which records the completion and returns the result to your browser.
  5. If the generation fails, the reserved credit is returned to your balance.

4. AI processing and third-party AI providers

To generate results, the text you submit is sent to Groq, which runs the AI models we use. Groq processes that content under its own terms and privacy policy, and we do not control how it handles data on its side. Please do not enter passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information into the tool.

6. Payments

We do not collect or store your card or bank details. Checkout and payment processing are handled by our payment provider, which acts as Merchant of Record. Our records of a purchase are limited to what is described in section 2.

7. Cookies, sessions and browser storage

Our code does not set its own advertising or analytics cookies. The service uses your browser’s storage for the following:

  • Sign-in session (local storage, managed by the Supabase library) so you stay signed in.
  • Generation history (local storage): your most recent generations, up to 10, kept only in your browser on that device.
  • Credit and Pro display cache (local storage): for display only. The server is authoritative.
  • License key (local storage): if you activate a license key manually, it is kept in your browser on that device.
  • Google sign-in flag (session storage): a temporary flag used while you return from Google sign-in.

Signing out clears the display cache for credits and Pro status. You can remove all remaining stored data at any time by clearing your browser’s site data. Third-party scripts we load (for example checkout or affiliate scripts) may use their own cookies or storage under their own policies.

8. Data retention

We have not set fixed retention periods for every type of record. In general: account records are kept while your account exists; credit, license and usage records are kept for as long as needed to operate your account, prevent duplicate charges or misuse, and handle purchases and refund requests; completed generation records are currently kept until we delete them; feedback emails remain in our mailbox until we delete them. Data in your own browser stays there until you clear it. You can ask us to delete your data (see section 10).

9. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, including: connections to the service over HTTPS; verifying your sign-in token on our server for credit-related actions; keeping credit balances on the server rather than trusting your browser; storing license keys only as one-way hashes; and keeping our secret keys on the server rather than in your browser. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We do not claim any security certification.

10. Your choices and privacy requests

You can ask us to tell you what account information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing tagpulse.ai@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we may keep records we are required to keep or need for security, fraud prevention or to resolve disputes. Depending on where you live, local law may give you additional rights, and we will consider requests made under those laws. You can sign out at any time and clear your browser storage yourself.
